Tarvin for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Tarvin records mileage and related work and vehicle information on your phone. It has no Tarvin account and does not run a server that receives your trip history. The limited outside services used by the Android app are described below.

Information on your phone

  • Location and routes: With your permission, Tarvin uses precise location to measure drives, draw routes, and estimate mileage deductions. If you enable automatic tracking, it can access location in the background to detect and record a drive while the app is closed. You can pause automatic tracking or change location permission in Android settings.
  • Motion and car Bluetooth: With your permission, activity recognition and the connection to a car Bluetooth device can help detect when a drive starts or ends. Tarvin stores the car device you select for that trigger.
  • Trips and entries: The app stores your classifications, vehicles, expenses, earnings, reminders, and derived reports in its local database.
  • Receipt images: If you choose a receipt image, Tarvin may keep a reference to it with the expense and use on-device text recognition to suggest fields. The app does not upload the image for recognition.

Information sent outside the app

  • Place names: When you view trip details, the Android geocoding service may receive the trip's start and end coordinates to return street or place names. The service is supplied by the device's geocoding provider, which may be Google or the device maker. Tarvin does not send your full route to its own server.
  • VIN lookup: If you request a vehicle lookup, the VIN you enter is sent to the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's vPIC service. This does not happen automatically.
  • Purchases: When in-app purchases are available, Google Play processes the payment and RevenueCat processes subscription status. RevenueCat may receive purchase history and technical information needed to manage your entitlement. Tarvin does not send your trip routes, expenses, or receipt images to RevenueCat.

Tarvin does not use an advertising identifier or an advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. It does not sell your data.

Backups and sharing

You can save a backup file and restore it later. The file contains your trips, earnings, expenses, vehicles, and related entries. You choose where to save it, including any cloud storage provider you select through Android's file picker. Tarvin does not currently provide automatic Google Drive backup or sync. You control any copies you export or share.

Deleting information

Use Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete all local data to erase the app's local records, or uninstall the app. Delete exported backup files from wherever you saved them separately. If you used a subscription and want associated RevenueCat customer data deleted, email tarvin.admin@gmail.com.

Children

Tarvin is intended for people who drive for work. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with a new date. For privacy or support questions, email tarvin.admin@gmail.com.
