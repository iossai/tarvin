Tarvin

Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

Tarvin has no account system, no servers, and no analytics. Your drives, vehicles, expenses and earnings are stored on your iPhone. The developer cannot see them — not as a promise, but because they are never received.

What Tarvin records

All of the following is recorded on your device, and only because a feature you are using needs it.

  • Location. Used to measure the distance of a drive and to draw its route. Tarvin asks for “Always” access so a drive can record itself while your phone is locked and in your pocket — that is the whole point of automatic tracking. Location is recorded during a drive, and to notice that one has started.
  • Motion activity. Used to detect when driving begins and ends, so a trip does not have to be started by hand.
  • The name of your car's audio system. If you use the Bluetooth trigger, Tarvin stores the name your car reports (for example “HandsFreeLink”) so it can recognise that you are in that vehicle. It does not scan for or record other Bluetooth devices around you.
  • Trips, vehicles, expenses and earnings. What you enter, and what is derived from a recorded drive.
  • Receipt photographs. Only if you choose to scan one. The image is stored alongside the expense it belongs to.

Where it is stored

On your device, in Tarvin's own private storage.

If you turn on iCloud Sync, a copy is mirrored into your personal iCloud account, in a private database that belongs to you. It is covered by Apple's privacy terms and by your Apple Account. The developer has no access to it and no way to request access.

iCloud Sync is off unless you turn it on.

What leaves your device

Three things, and none of them go to Tarvin — there is no Tarvin server for anything to be sent to.

Place names for your trips

To label where a drive started and ended, Tarvin asks Apple's geocoding service to turn coordinates into a place name. Those coordinates are sent to Apple and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. This is the one case where location data leaves the device, and it is worth stating plainly rather than burying.

Your subscription

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Tarvin is told only whether a subscription is currently active. It never sees your name, payment details, or Apple Account.

A vehicle identification number, if you look one up

Pit Stop can fill in a vehicle's year, make and model from its VIN. If you use that feature, the VIN alone is sent to vPIC, the free public vehicle database run by the United States National Highway Traffic Safety Administration. Nothing identifying you or your device is sent with it.

This only happens when you tap “Look Up Vehicle” or scan a VIN plate. It never runs on its own, and typing the year, make and model yourself works just as well.

That is the complete list.

What Tarvin never collects

  • No name, email address, phone number or password — there is no account to create.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services.
  • No third-party trackers or software development kits of any kind. The VIN lookup above is a direct request to a public government database, not an embedded service.
  • Your data is never sold, rented, or shared. There is no one to share it with.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are created on your device by the app itself — a warning that automatic detection has switched off, a maintenance reminder, or your end-of-day check-in. Nothing is delivered through a remote push server.

Deleting your data

Deleting Tarvin from your device removes everything stored on it.

If you turned on iCloud Sync, the synced copy is removed separately: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Tarvin.

Children

Tarvin is intended for people who drive for work. It is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Because Tarvin holds no contact details for you, this page is the only place a change is published.
