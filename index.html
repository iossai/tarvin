Common questions

A drive was not recorded automatically

Automatic detection needs Always location access. If location is set to “While Using the App”, Tarvin can measure a drive you start by hand but cannot notice one on its own. Check Settings → Tarvin → Location.

Also confirm Auto-Detect is switched on inside the app. If you paused it, Tarvin will tell you — provided notifications are allowed.

Starting a trip when the car connects

Tarvin can begin recording when your phone connects to your car's audio system. Pick that trigger in the app's settings while you are connected to the car, so it can learn which device is yours.

My subscription is not showing on another device

Subscriptions follow the Apple Account that bought them. Sign in to the same account, open Tarvin, and Pro should appear on its own. If it does not, use Restore Purchases in the app's membership screen.

Moving to a new phone

Turn on iCloud Sync before you switch. Without it, your drives live only on the device that recorded them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything on the device. If iCloud Sync was on, remove the synced copy in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Tarvin.

Privacy

Tarvin has no account, no servers, and no analytics. Your drives stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud Sync, in which case they go to your own private iCloud database.

The full detail, including the one case where location data reaches Apple, is in the Privacy Policy.
